How it works

Four steps, on a loop.

Engine one

2.1% each way, and none of it is ours

2.1% on every KLY buy, 2.1% on every sell. Not a unit of it reaches a team wallet. It market-buys the tokens the DAO holds, split by their market cap.

On 1,000 USDC of KLY volume: 21 USDC of fees, every unit spent buying the index: . Bigger token, bigger share, recalculated every epoch.

Volume is a real engine but not a promise: no volume, no buying. Bonding and treasury operations are exempt, otherwise the protocol would tax itself.

Engine two

Bonds, in plain words

A bond is a swap with a bonus, and the only way new KLY is ever created.

You giveYour ARGUS, RAT or USDC

Any amount, straight from your wallet.

→
You get backMore KLY than you handed over

Released in a steady drip over five days, not instantly.

Why does the DAO pay more than it gets?

Because it keeps what you hand over forever instead of renting liquidity for a week. The bonus is the price of owning something permanently.

Why the five-day wait?

So nobody can take the bonus and dump it in the same block. You are paid for patience, not for speed.

What is the catch?

The price can move against you during those five days. The bonus is only worth taking if you wanted to hold KLY anyway.

Engine three

Borrow against your bag. Do not sell it.

Bonding is permanent: you hand the token over and it is gone. A loan is the other door. Pledge the same token, take USDC out, keep every bit of the upside, pay it back whenever you like.

400 USDCyou can draw today, at 40% loan to value
60 USDCinterest for a full year, at 15% on what you drew

Your position is liquidated only if the collateral falls 38% from here, and the interest you pay goes into the same split as everything else the vault earns.

Liquidation is acquisition

A normal lender dumps your collateral on the market and craters the token. KLYRA keeps it: liquidated collateral is exactly what the treasury was trying to buy anyway.

No term, no schedule

Interest accrues per block. Repay tomorrow or in two years, partially or in full. Nothing is called in as long as the ratio holds.

Paid in USDC

Interest arrives in the unit the vault reports in, and flows into the same three-way split as the farm income.

The point

This is a machine for farming $ARC

Be honest about the assets: ARGUS and RAT do not pay a dividend. Held in a wallet they produce nothing at all. The return has to come from what you do with them, and on a young chain there is one thing worth doing.

Pair them

Every token the treasury takes in gets paired with USDC and deployed as a liquidity position on Arc.

Leave them

The DAO never withdraws. That liquidity is permanent, which is exactly the kind a chain pays to attract.

Farm ARC

Those positions earn trading fees and, while the programme runs, ARC incentives. That is the yield.
